I don’t have a good name for this but I think I want to scratch my own itch and make a tracking/reminder/calendar app. I am constantly trying to find new ways to develop a better relationship with my phone, and apps are all about monetization these days. I want to make a great mobile app that has the functionality and customizability of high end mobile apps, but also make it free to use, and therefore free to quit.

# Inspirations:  
\- Lock-In: Pay to use \- track everything \- XP System  
\-Opal: Free to use \- has reported bugs \- only screen time \- calendar routines

# My own Itch:  
\- Goals centered  
\- Structure your day  
\- Avoid addictive sites  
\- Gamify the process  
\- Provide barriers to addictive sites that actually work  
\- Social features to promote accountability

# Idea:  
Award XP points for getting the things done that you need in the day. For every 10 XP you are allowed on the mobile sites of your choice on your phone. I would choose instagram, youtube, tiktok, etc.

When you start the app you get to choose how much time you want to allot yourself to these apps, be it 1, 2, 3 hours, etc. The app then calculates that amount of time into XP

(1 hour \= 600 XP, 2 hours \= 1200 XP)

Then when you plan out your day, you get to choose which things are worth the XP, and you can spread it out to multiple tasks throughout the day. Say I need to do the dishes, laundry, and vacuum, then I would put those reminders in and make them worth however much XP I want out of my allotment. The important thing after this, is that those apps get locked after their time is up. Regular IOS apps allow a “15 more minutes” feature, which destroys the whole point of a screen time barrier.

Another thing I would want to promote is social accountability. Going along with the theme of “Gamifying the Process”, the XP points that you don’t end up spending would be saved to your public profile, then you and your friends can set up a competition on who has the highest amount of XP by the end of the week or month or so. Basically making a game out of avoiding addictive doomscrolling platforms.  
Aesthetic:  
\-Colors: Mainly black and white with shades of gray in between  
\-Fonts: I want this to have a distinguished feeling to it, so I would go with either Times New Roman or Serif.

# Pages:

1. Main Hub

\-This would be the primary page that opens when you open the app. It will show you a dashboard version of your goals, progress, and amount of XP that you’ve gained for that day.

\-It’s meant to be minimalistic and a broad reminder of what the next task is.

2. Calendar and Goals

\-This Page is where you set up the goals you have, plan out your day / week, and notify your friends that you’ve set your goals

\-It will be a bit more full since it will include elements like the calendar and goal setting on the same page.

3. Tracking

\-This Page is dedicated to showing your progress for recurring goals, progress on completing all goals in a day, and enduring XP which is earned through completing goals and yet not spending all of your screen time with that XP. 

\-The style of this page should be fairly customizable. I prefer to look at my progress through a Line graph for recurring goals, but bar graphs are also useful. In the top left, there should be a distinction from the Main Hub, since the enduring xp is different from daily earned xp. Another added feature could be comparing you and a friend's goal, if you share it.

4. Account and Social Elements

\-This final page is a simple account page, which could also house settings and social features. This app is not meant to be a social media platform and therefore will not recommend people to friend. The way they should be able to connect with people is through a direct link of some kind.
